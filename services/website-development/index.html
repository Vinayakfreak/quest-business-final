What a business website should cover

  • Clear service pages with unique descriptions and real deliverables
  • Mobile-friendly layout, accessible navigation and readable content
  • Visible enquiry options and forms that actually deliver submissions
  • Fast, appropriately sized images and minimal unnecessary scripts
  • SEO titles, meta descriptions, canonical URLs and crawlable internal links
  • Sitemap, robots directives and Search Console setup where access is available

Our build process

  • Agree on goals, pages, content, assets and integrations
  • Create page structure and visual direction
  • Build responsive pages and test links, forms and mobile layouts
  • Review technical SEO and basic accessibility
  • Publish after approval and monitor for errors

Before launch

  • Confirm domain ownership, hosting and form delivery
  • Add appropriate privacy notice, consent language and analytics permissions
  • Test enquiry submissions; a page that looks finished but loses form messages is not a working lead-generation website
