Make customer follow-up more consistent on WhatsApp.

WhatsApp can be a useful customer communication channel when messages are relevant and expected. We help businesses plan workflows for incoming enquiries, reminders and follow-up without treating every contact as permission for unlimited promotions.

Discuss your project

Useful WhatsApp workflows

  • Welcome and enquiry acknowledgement messages
  • FAQ responses and routing to a human when needed
  • Appointment confirmations and reminders
  • Lead follow-up based on the customer's request and consent
  • Order or service updates where applicable
  • Conversation handoff and internal notifications

Setup checklist

  • Define message purpose, audience and consent collection
  • Select appropriate WhatsApp Business tools or an approved provider
  • Write concise templates and escalation rules
  • Test delivery, opt-outs, edge cases and human handoff
  • Measure response time, resolved enquiries and qualified leads

Trust and platform compliance

  • Use approved messaging methods, respect opt-outs and follow WhatsApp policies and applicable privacy rules
  • Do not purchase contact lists or send unsolicited bulk messages
  • Automation should make service more useful, not create spam
